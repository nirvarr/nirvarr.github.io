About

I am a data scientist working on computer vision and deep learning. I recently completed a Master of Data Science at Universiti Malaya (Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology), graduating with Distinction (CGPA 3.90/4.00) under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Norjihan Binti Abdul Ghani.

My master's thesis asked whether convolutional neural networks that classify golden berry (Physalis peruviana) ripeness learn the fruit or the photographic setting. On ordinary images, a shape-blind colour-histogram baseline came within 3 points of the best CNN. A background-randomisation control I designed separated them: the baseline fell to 82.5% while pretrained CNNs held 94.2%, widening ResNet50's margin from 2.9 to 11.7 points. Headline accuracy on mixed-source images, in other words, can overstate what a model has actually learned.

Before my master's, I earned a BSc in Computer Science from American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). I am now looking for a fully funded PhD position on robustness and shortcut learning in visual classification.
