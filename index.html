About

Hello! I'm Nirvar, a machine learning researcher from Dhaka, Bangladesh. I recently completed my Master of Data Science at Universiti Malaya, graduating with Distinction (CGPA 3.90/4.00) under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Norjihan Binti Abdul Ghani.

My master's thesis examined background bias in CNN-based ripeness classification of golden berries: whether a model judges the fruit, or the scene around it. Before that, I studied Computer Science at American International University-Bangladesh, labelled and segmented images for a content-moderation classifier at Netstar (SG), and co-authored a study of LLM code generation published at NSysS 2024.

I am now applying for PhD positions in computer vision, model robustness and evaluation methodology, and I would be glad to hear from groups working in these areas.
